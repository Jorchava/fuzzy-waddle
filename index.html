What is color contrast accessibility?

Color contrast accessibility ensures text remains readable for users with low vision, color blindness, or visual impairments.

What WCAG contrast ratio is required?

WCAG 2.1 AA requires a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for normal text.
